Dabao Workbench

by BlocksIndia

Check, test and sign with your Baochip-1x Dabao board, straight from the browser. Nothing is installed: the page talks to the board over USB, in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Test network only. Not audited. This is a tool for developers. With the signer firmware, the key in the board cannot be backed up, the board has no PIN, and it cannot show what it signs. Do not use it for anything of value.

1. Check your board any Dabao

The first code the chip runs is its bootloader. This reads the fingerprints (SHA-512) the bootloader reports for its two stages and compares them with builds that were rebuilt from the public source. It works on a board as it comes, with no firmware of ours.

Put the board in its bootloader: hold PROG, tap RESET, release PROG (a drive named BAOCHIP shows up). Then click "Read and check" and pick the board's serial port.

This browser has no Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

The two quieter buttons are for a board running the signer firmware (section 2): they move it into the bootloader and back without touching the board.

2. Connect the signer needs the signer firmware

This browser has no WebHID. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

The rest of this page talks to the touch-to-confirm Ethereum signer, open firmware you can build and flash yourself: source and build steps.

Plug the board in and start the signer (press PROG if it is waiting in its bootloader). Then pick "Dabao HID" in the dialog. Keep this page open in one tab only.
