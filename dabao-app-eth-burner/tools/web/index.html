Check your board any Dabao

The first code the chip runs is its bootloader. This reads the fingerprints (SHA-512) the bootloader reports for its two stages and compares them with builds that were rebuilt from the public source. It works on a board as it comes, with no firmware of ours.

Put the board in its bootloader: hold PROG, tap RESET, release PROG (a drive named BAOCHIP shows up). Then click "Read and check" and pick the board's serial port.

This browser has no Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

The two quieter buttons are for a board running the signer firmware: they move it into the bootloader and back without touching the board.

Know the limits

    
Test network only. Not audited. This is a tool for developers. With the signer firmware, the key in the board cannot be backed up, the board has no PIN, and it cannot show what it signs. Do not use it for anything of value.

    
The drawing of the board is a guide to where things are, not an exact picture.
